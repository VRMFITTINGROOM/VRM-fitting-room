Tips and how it works

100% is the original size. Double-click a slider label to reset it. Drag on the preview to orbit and scroll to zoom.

Belly, chest, hips, thighs, calves and arm thickness reshape the body surface in a smooth band around that body part, so the rest of the character does not stretch. Height, leg length, arm length and head size rescale bones. Textures, materials, blendshapes and the skeleton layout are kept.

Hair, skirt and other spring-bone physics keep their original settings. After large changes, check them in your VRM viewer. Values within about 40% of the original give the cleanest result.
