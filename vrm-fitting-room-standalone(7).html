Tips and how it works

100% is the original size. Double-click a slider label to reset it, or use the Reset link beside a section title. Click a body part in the preview to zoom to it and highlight its sliders. Click empty space or press Esc to zoom back out. Jiggle mode lets you touch or drag on the body to see it wobble; this is only a preview and is not saved in the export. Drag to orbit and scroll to zoom.

Belly, chest, hips, butt, thighs, calves, arm thickness and the chin sliders reshape the body surface in a smooth band around that body part, so the rest of the character does not stretch. Height, leg length, arm length and head size rescale bones. Textures, materials, blendshapes and the skeleton layout are kept.

Hair, skirt and other spring-bone physics keep their original settings. After large changes, check them in your VRM viewer. Values within about 40% of the original give the cleanest result.
